# WellaWhite Site Navigation

Use this document to navigate the static WellaWhite Water Flosser information site at https://get-wellawhite.tenstarshub.com/.

## Product
WellaWhite Water Flosser is presented as a rechargeable water flosser for targeted dental hygiene. The site documents water-jet cleaning, three cleaning modes, interchangeable nozzles, use around braces and dental appliances, battery use, operation, maintenance and FAQ.
